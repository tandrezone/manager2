The change, in one screen

How the order runs today

  • Installer photographs a handwritten schedule of sizes and emails it.
  • Someone prices it by hand from the rate card, into a spreadsheet.
  • Quote sits in an inbox. Two days later it is accepted, with a change.
  • Sizes are retyped into production. This is where the scrap comes from.
  • Delivery date agreed verbally. Invoice raised separately at month end.

How it runs here

  • Installer enters sizes once, on site, and sees the price immediately.
  • Rate card applied consistently, at their tier, at any hour.
  • Order arrives validated against what the plant can make.
  • Sizes are never retyped. Whoever measured owns the measurement.
  • Window chosen at checkout. Invoice raised on dispatch, correctly.

Every figure on this page is illustrative sample data for demonstration. Rates, capacities and performance values are configured per manufacturer. Insulating glass units are CE-marked construction products: EN 1279-5 applies, and the Construction Products Regulation requires a manufacturer to identify, on request and for ten years, the customers it supplied — which is why this portal is built around verified trade accounts rather than anonymous logins.
